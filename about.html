My Story

Why I teach English

I'm Brian Macapobre, a professional English teacher based in the Philippines with over three years of experience teaching English to students around the world. I hold a TEFL certificate and a PRC teaching license, and for the past few years I've taught online with an international ESL company — mostly working with kids and teens learning to speak English for the first time, or trying to build the confidence to actually use it.

Most of my students start out shy. They know some grammar, they can read a little, but the moment they have to actually speak, they freeze. That's the part I care about most — not another worksheet, but a student who's genuinely willing to try, make mistakes, and keep talking.

I started teaching independently because I wanted to build real, longer-term relationships with students and their families, instead of being one of many teachers a student sees for 25 minutes and never again. Here, you're working with the same teacher every time, someone who actually remembers your child's progress.

My Approach

How I teach

Speaking first

Lessons are built around getting your child talking — describing pictures, answering real questions, telling small stories — with grammar corrected gently along the way.

Patient with beginners

I don't rush a student who's still finding their words. Confidence comes before fluency, and confidence takes patience.

Personalized, not scripted

I adjust each lesson to what your child actually needs that week, rather than following a fixed company curriculum start to finish.

Consistent scheduling

I teach across time zones and keep a regular weekly slot for returning students, so lessons become a routine, not a scramble.
